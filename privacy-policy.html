Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-20 · Effective date: 2026-11-20

1. Overview

This privacy policy explains how Jangwa Uploader ("the App"), a personal automation tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel จังหวะ JANGWA (@jangwadaily), handles data. The App is used by a single person (the owner) and is not offered as a public service.

2. What data the App accesses

With the owner's explicit OAuth consent, the App uses the YouTube Data API to access the owner's YouTube channel and to upload videos that the owner has explicitly created and chosen to publish. The App may read limited metadata about the owner's own uploads (for example, video ID and status). The App does not access any other Google user's data.

3. How the App uses data

Data is used solely to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to record a local log of publishing activity so the App does not upload the same video twice.

The App does not sell, rent, transfer, or share data with any third party. No data is used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App's use of data received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the functionality described in this policy.

5. Data storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's own computer in protected files (file permissions 0600) and are never published. The local publishing log is stored on the owner's computer. Data is retained only as long as needed for the App's operation, and the owner may revoke the App's access at any time from Google Account permissions.

6. Security

The App runs locally on the owner's computer. Credentials are not transmitted to any server controlled by the App; requests are made directly to Google's APIs over HTTPS.

7. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the owner of the จังหวะ JANGWA channel, who operates this App.
